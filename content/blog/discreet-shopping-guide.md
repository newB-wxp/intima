# Your Guide to Discreet Shopping: Privacy from Click to Delivery

At Intima, we understand that privacy is not a luxury — it is a requirement. Whether you share a mailbox, live with family, or simply value your personal boundaries, we have designed every step of your shopping experience with discretion in mind. Here is exactly how your privacy is protected.

## While You Browse

**No explicit imagery.** Our website features elegant product photography on neutral backgrounds. You can browse Intima in any setting without drawing attention.

**Discreet browser tab.** Our page titles use neutral language. The browser tab reads "Intima Wellness" — never anything more descriptive.

**No retargeting ads.** We do not use retargeting pixels that would show Intima ads following you around the internet. Your browsing history stays private.

## At Checkout

**Neutral billing descriptor.** Your credit card or PayPal statement will show a generic billing name — "INTIMA WELLNESS" or similar — never the full company name or product references. We partner with CCBill and Stripe, both of which offer discreet billing options specifically designed for privacy-sensitive purchases.

**No account required.** You can check out as a guest. We never require you to create an account or save your payment information. If you do create an account for order tracking, your data is encrypted and never shared.

**SSL encryption.** Every page on Intima uses HTTPS encryption. Your personal information, payment details, and order history are protected by the same security standards used by banks.

## At Delivery

This is where our commitment to discretion really shows:

**Plain packaging.** Every Intima order ships in a plain brown corrugated box with zero external branding. No logos, no product names, no company name. The box is completely anonymous.

**Discreet return address.** The shipping label shows the sender as "Intima" or "I Fulfillment" — not "Intima Wellness" or anything referencing personal care. Even the return address is carefully chosen for privacy.

**No packing slip descriptions.** The packing slip inside uses product codes, not product names. Even if someone opens your package, they will see generic references — not descriptive language.

**Double-boxed for fragile items.** Premium products ship in an inner presentation box, inside the plain outer shipping box. The outer box is the only thing anyone sees.

> Learn more on our [Shipping Policy](/shipping) page.

## Payment Privacy

**Multiple payment methods.** We accept credit cards, PayPal, and cryptocurrency through our payment partners. PayPal adds an extra layer of privacy by keeping your payment details separate from the merchant.

**No stored credit cards.** We never store full credit card numbers on our servers. Payment processing is handled entirely by Stripe and CCBill, both PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant — the highest security standard in the payment industry.

- "I found this brand called Intima that has really tasteful, body-safe products. Would you be open to looking at their site with me?"
