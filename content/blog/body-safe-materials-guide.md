# The Complete Guide to Body-Safe Materials

When it comes to personal wellness devices, the material matters — not just for comfort, but for your health. At Intima, every product we carry is made from body-safe, non-porous materials. Here is what you need to know before making your choice.

## Medical-Grade Silicone: The Gold Standard

Medical-grade silicone is the most popular body-safe material — and for good reason. It is hypoallergenic, non-porous (meaning bacteria cannot hide in microscopic holes), and remarkably soft to the touch. Look for products labeled "100% platinum-cure silicone" or "FDA-certified silicone." These have passed rigorous testing for biocompatibility.

Avoid anything labeled simply "silicone blend" or "sil-a-gel" — these are often mixed with cheaper fillers that degrade over time.

**Pros:** Soft, flexible, body-safe, easy to clean, retains warmth  
**Cons:** Cannot be used with silicone-based lubricants (use water-based only)

## Borosilicate Glass: Beauty Meets Function

Glass massagers are hand-crafted from borosilicate glass — the same material used in laboratory equipment and high-end cookware. They are non-porous, hypoallergenic, and completely body-safe. Because glass is temperature-responsive, you can warm or cool your device for a customized experience.

**Pros:** Hypoallergenic, temperature-play compatible, visually stunning, extremely durable  
**Cons:** Heavier than silicone, requires careful handling

## Surgical-Grade Stainless Steel: Built for Life

Stainless steel massagers offer unmatched durability and a satisfying weight that many users prefer. Like glass, they are temperature-responsive and compatible with all lubricant types. Surgical-grade (316L) steel is the same material used in medical implants.

**Pros:** Lifetime durability, all lubricant compatible, weighted feel, temperature-play ready  
**Cons:** Heaviest option, premium price point

## TPE and TPR: Know the Risks

Thermoplastic elastomers (TPE) and thermoplastic rubber (TPR) are porous materials commonly found in budget devices. While they feel soft, their porous nature means they can harbor bacteria even after cleaning. We do not recommend these materials for intimate use. If you own a TPE device, it should be replaced every 6–8 months.

## ABS Plastic: Safe for External Use

ABS plastic is a hard, non-porous material often used for external massagers and accessory components. It is body-safe and easy to clean, though not as luxurious as silicone or glass.

## How to Spot Counterfeit Materials

Unfortunately, the market has its share of mislabeled products. Here are three quick tests:

1. **The Flame Test (Silicone):** Real silicone does not melt or emit black smoke when exposed to flame. TPE melts and smells like burnt plastic. *Only perform this test on a small, inconspicuous area.*
2. **The Water Test (Glass):** Authentic borosilicate glass has no seams or bubbles. Run your finger along the surface — it should feel perfectly smooth.
3. **The Magnet Test (Steel):** Surgical-grade 316L stainless steel is non-magnetic. If a magnet sticks, it is likely a lower-grade alloy.
