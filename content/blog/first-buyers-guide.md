# Your First Personal Massager: A Complete Buying Guide

Buying your first personal wellness device can feel overwhelming. With so many shapes, sizes, materials, and features, where do you even begin? This guide will walk you through every decision — from budget to features — so you can shop with confidence.

## Step 1: Define Your Budget

Personal massagers range from $25 to $200+. As a general rule, investing more means getting better materials, longer battery life, quieter motors, and a proper warranty. Here is a rough breakdown:

| Price Range | What to Expect |
|-------------|----------------|
| $25–$50 | Basic features, may use TPE (porous), shorter lifespan |
| $50–$100 | Body-safe silicone, decent battery, 1-year warranty |
| $100–$150 | Premium silicone, quiet motor, travel lock, 2-year warranty |
| $150+ | Luxury materials (glass/metal), artisan design, extended warranty |

For a first device, we recommend the $50–$100 range. You will get body-safe materials and reliable performance without overcommitting. [Browse our Best Sellers](/category/best-sellers) in this sweet spot.

## Step 2: Choose Your Material

**For beginners, medical-grade silicone is almost always the right choice.** It is soft, forgiving, body-safe, and easy to maintain. Glass and metal are excellent upgrades once you know your preferences, but they are heavier and more expensive.

Read our [Complete Guide to Body-Safe Materials](/blog/body-safe-materials-guide) for a deep dive.

## Step 3: Consider These Key Features

### Size and Shape
Start with something modest. Compact, ergonomic designs are easier to handle and store. Bullet-style or palm-sized massagers are popular first choices. If you are unsure, look for products labeled "beginner-friendly."

> Our [Beginner Friendly](/category/beginner-friendly) collection is curated specifically for first-time users.

### Noise Level
If privacy is a concern — whether you live with roommates, family, or in an apartment with thin walls — pay attention to decibel ratings. Devices under 45dB are considered whisper-quiet. Many of our [Quiet Massagers](/category/quiet-massagers) operate below this threshold.

### Waterproofing
An IPX7 waterproof rating means the device can be fully submerged in water. This makes cleaning easier and opens up bath or shower use. Even if you do not plan to use it in water, waterproof devices are more hygienic because there are no seams for moisture to seep into.

### Rechargeable vs. Battery
USB rechargeable devices are more eco-friendly and cost-effective over time. Look for USB-C charging — it is faster and more convenient than older magnetic chargers. Our [USB Rechargeable](/category/usb-rechargeable) collection eliminates disposable batteries entirely.

## Step 4: Do Not Forget the Accessories

A good water-based lubricant is essential. Silicone-based lubricants can degrade silicone massagers, so stick with water-based. A storage pouch or case keeps your device clean between uses. And a dedicated toy cleaner makes maintenance effortless.

## The Most Important Rule

There is no "wrong" choice — only the choice that is right for *you*. Take your time, read reviews, and trust your instincts. Your wellness journey is personal, and Intima is here to support you every step of the way.
