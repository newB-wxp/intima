Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 15, 2026

At Intima Wellness ("Intima," "we," "us," or "our"), your privacy is foundational to everything we do. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website or make a purchase.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide when you:

  • Create an account: Name and email address.
  • Place an order: Name, email address, shipping address, billing address, and phone number.
  • Subscribe to marketing: Email address for newsletters and promotional communications.
  • Contact customer support: Any information you choose to share in your inquiry.

Payment Information: We do not store full credit card numbers, CVV codes, or bank account details on our servers. All payment processing is handled by our PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment partners: Stripe and CCBill. Your payment data is transmitted directly to these processors via encrypted connections and is never accessible to Intima staff.

We also collect certain information automatically when you visit our site, including IP address, browser type, device information, pages viewed, and referring URL. This data is collected via cookies and similar technologies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • Order Processing: To fulfill and ship your orders, send order confirmations, and provide tracking information.
  • Customer Support: To respond to your inquiries, process returns, and resolve issues.
  • Marketing Communications: To send promotional emails about new products, special offers, and content. You may unsubscribe at any time via the link in any marketing email.
  • Site Improvement: To analyze how visitors use our website and improve the user experience.
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal requests.

3. Information Protection

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data:

  • SSL/TLS Encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using 256-bit SSL technology. You can verify this by the padlock icon in your browser's address bar.
  • Access Control: Personal data is accessible only to authorized personnel who require it for order fulfillment, customer support, or system maintenance.
  • Data Minimization: We collect only the information necessary to provide our services and retain it only as long as needed.
  • Regular Audits: Our security practices are reviewed periodically to ensure they meet or exceed industry standards.

4. Discreet Shipping & Privacy Packaging

We understand that privacy extends beyond data — it includes physical delivery. All Intima orders ship in:

  • Plain brown corrugated boxes with zero external branding — no logos, no product names, no company identifiers.
  • Shipping labels showing the sender as "Intima" — never the full company name or product references.
  • Internal packing slips using product codes only, not descriptive names.

For complete details, please see our Shipping Policy.

5. Cookie Policy

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to:

  • Keep items in your shopping cart between visits.
  • Remember your preferences and site settings.
  • Analyze site traffic and usage patterns (via Google Analytics).
  • Prevent fraudulent activity and improve security.

You can control cookie preferences through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain site functionality, including the shopping cart and checkout process.

6. Third-Party Services

We share information with trusted third parties only as necessary to operate our business:

  • Payment Processors (Stripe, CCBill): To process payments securely. These providers are PCI-DSS compliant and adhere to strict data protection standards.
  • Shipping Carriers (USPS, UPS, FedEx, DHL): To deliver your orders. We share only the shipping address and contact information required for delivery.
  • Analytics (Google Analytics): To understand site usage. IP addresses are anonymized. You can opt out via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
  • Email Service Provider: To send transactional emails and marketing communications.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for their marketing purposes.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Deletion: Request that we delete your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements.
  • Portability: Request your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address listed below. We will respond within 30 days as required by applicable law.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Intima Wellness is intended exclusively for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect, use, or disclose personal information from anyone under the age of 18. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal data from a minor, we will delete it immediately. If you believe a minor has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

9. International Data Transfers

Intima is based in the United States. If you access our services from outside the US, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in the US. By using our services, you consent to this transfer in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Material changes will be communicated via email (if you have an account) or a prominent notice on our website. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

11. Contact Us
