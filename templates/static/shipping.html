Discreet Packaging

Privacy is central to our shipping practices. Every Intima order — regardless of destination, order size, or shipping method — ships in 100% discreet packaging:

  • Outer Box: Plain brown corrugated cardboard box. No logos, branding, product names, or company identifiers of any kind appear on the exterior.
  • Return Address: The shipping label shows the sender as simply "Intima" — never "Intima Wellness" or any descriptive variant. The return address is a neutral fulfillment address.
  • Internal Packing Slip: Uses product codes (e.g., "IM-1042") rather than product names. Even if the box is opened by someone else, the contents are not identifiable by the packing materials.
  • Product Packaging: Products themselves arrive in elegant but unbranded inner packaging — discreet enough to leave on a nightstand without drawing unwanted attention.

We have deliberately designed every layer of our packaging to prioritize your privacy. There are no exceptions to our discreet packaging policy.

Order Tracking

Once your order ships, you will receive a shipping confirmation email containing your tracking number and a link to track your package in real time. You can also track your order by logging into your Intima account and navigating to your order history.

Tracking information typically becomes available within 24 hours of shipment. If your tracking number shows no movement after 48 hours, please contact us at support@intima-wellness.com.

Customs, Duties, and Taxes

For international orders (outside the United States):

  • Import duties, customs fees, and local taxes may be charged by your destination country upon delivery. These charges are not included in the product price or shipping cost at checkout and are the responsibility of the recipient.
  • Customs policies vary widely by country. We recommend contacting your local customs office for information about potential charges before placing your order.
  • Intima is not responsible for delays caused by customs processing. On rare occasions, customs may hold packages for inspection, which can add several days to the delivery estimate.
  • If a package is refused or returned due to unpaid customs fees, any refund will be processed minus return shipping costs and any fees incurred by Intima.

Shipping Carriers

We partner with trusted carriers to ensure reliable delivery:

  • Domestic (US): USPS, UPS, and FedEx.
  • International: DHL, UPS, and USPS (via local postal partners in destination country).

The carrier for your order is selected based on destination, service level, and package characteristics to ensure the most reliable delivery. All carriers are instructed to deliver packages without requiring a signature unless signature confirmation is specifically requested at checkout.

Lost or Damaged Packages

  • Lost Packages: If your tracking shows delivery but you have not received your package, first check with neighbors, building management, or your local post office. If the package is still missing after 48 hours, contact us. We will initiate a carrier investigation and, if the package is confirmed lost, send a replacement or issue a refund.
  • Damaged Packages: If your package arrives visibly damaged, please photograph the exterior before opening, and photograph any damaged items inside. Send these photos to support@intima-wellness.com within 48 hours of delivery. We will arrange a replacement or refund.

Incorrect Addresses

Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. If you realize you have entered an incorrect address, contact us immediately at support@intima-wellness.com. If the order has not yet shipped, we can update the address. If it has already shipped, we will do our best to intercept the package, but we cannot guarantee successful redirection.

Packages returned to us due to incorrect or incomplete addresses will be refunded minus original and return shipping costs.

Questions?

For any shipping-related questions not covered here, reach out to our support team at support@intima-wellness.com. We respond to all inquiries within 24 hours on business days.
