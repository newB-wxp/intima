Frequently Asked Questions

Are your products body-safe?

Absolutely. All Intima products are made from medical-grade silicone, tempered glass, or stainless steel — materials that are non-porous, hypoallergenic, and free from phthalates, BPA, and latex. Every product undergoes rigorous third-party testing to meet medical-grade safety standards before it reaches you.

How do I clean my products?

Clean your products before and after each use with warm water and mild, fragrance-free soap. For silicone and glass products, you can also use a dedicated toy cleaner. Avoid harsh chemicals, bleach, or abrasive sponges. For rechargeable products, ensure the charging port is fully sealed before washing. Always dry thoroughly before storing.

Is the packaging discreet?

Yes. Every order ships in a plain brown box with no external logos, branding, or any indication of the contents. The return address label reads a neutral company name — not "Intima." Even our credit card statements use a discreet billing descriptor. Your privacy is our priority at every step.

How long does shipping take?

Standard shipping within the United States takes 3-7 business days. Express shipping (2-3 business days) is available at checkout. International orders to the EU typically arrive within 7-14 business days. For other international destinations, please allow 10-21 business days. You'll receive a tracking number as soon as your order ships.

What is your return policy?

For health and hygiene reasons, we can only accept returns on products that are unused, unopened, and in their original sealed packaging. You have 30 days from the date of delivery to initiate a return. If your product arrives damaged or defective, please contact us immediately and we'll arrange a replacement at no cost to you.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship to most countries worldwide. International shipping rates are calculated at checkout based on your location and order weight. Please note that international orders may be subject to customs duties and import taxes — these are the responsibility of the recipient. Contact our support team if you have questions about shipping to your country.

How do I track my order?

Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number. You can track your package anytime through the link in that email, or by logging into your Intima account and visiting your order history. If you haven't received tracking info within 3 business days, please check your spam folder or contact our support team.

Is payment secure?

Yes. Our checkout is protected by SSL encryption — the same level of security used by banks. We never store your full credit card details on our servers. All payment processing is handled by PCI-compliant payment partners. Your payment information is safe with us.

Can I cancel my order?

You can cancel your order within 2 hours of placing it by contacting our customer support team. After 2 hours, orders enter processing and cannot be canceled. If your order has already shipped, you'll need to follow our return policy once it arrives.

Do you have a warranty?

Yes. Every Intima product comes with a 1-year manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. If your product stops working or shows a manufacturing defect within one year of purchase, we'll repair or replace it at no charge. The warranty does not cover damage from misuse, unauthorized modifications, or normal wear and tear. To make a warranty claim, contact our support team with your order number.

Still have questions?

Our support team is here to help. We typically respond within 24 hours.
