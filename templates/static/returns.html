Return Policy

Last Updated: July 15, 2026

At Intima, we stand behind every product we sell. If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, we are here to help. Please read our return policy carefully before initiating a return.

30-Day Return Window

You may return eligible products within 30 calendar days from the date of delivery. To be eligible for a return, items must meet the conditions outlined below.

Eligibility Requirements

Eligible for Return

  • Products that are unopened and in original, sealed packaging.
  • Products that are unused and in the same condition as received.
  • Products returned with all original accessories, manuals, and packaging materials.
  • Defective or damaged products (see Quality Issues below).

Not Eligible for Return

  • Opened intimate products: For health and hygiene reasons, any personal massager or intimate care product that has been removed from its sealed packaging cannot be returned. This is an industry-standard policy and is non-negotiable.
  • Products returned more than 30 days after delivery.
  • Products not purchased directly from Intima Wellness (please contact the retailer where purchased).
  • Gift cards and digital products.
  • Items marked as "Final Sale" or "Clearance" at the time of purchase.

How to Initiate a Return

Step 1: Contact Customer Support

Email support@intima-wellness.com with your order number, the item(s) you wish to return, and the reason for return. Our team typically responds within 24 hours.

Step 2: Receive Your RMA Number

If your return is approved, you will receive a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) number and detailed return instructions. Returns sent without an RMA number cannot be processed.

Step 3: Package and Ship

Securely package the item(s) in their original packaging (if available) or equivalent protective packaging. Include the RMA number clearly on or inside the package. Ship to the address provided in your return instructions.

Step 4: Inspection and Refund

Once we receive and inspect your return (typically 2–3 business days after delivery to our warehouse), we will process your refund. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days. You will receive an email confirmation once the refund has been initiated.

Return Shipping Costs

  • Customer Remorse (changed mind, ordered wrong item): Return shipping costs are the responsibility of the customer. We recommend using a trackable shipping service, as we cannot guarantee receipt of returns shipped without tracking.
  • Defective or Incorrect Items: Intima covers return shipping for items that arrive defective, damaged, or incorrect. We will provide a prepaid return shipping label.

Quality Issues and Defective Products

We take product quality seriously. If your item arrives defective, damaged, or not as described:

  • Contact us within 7 days of delivery with photos or video documenting the issue.
  • We will assess the issue and offer either a replacement (same item, shipped at our expense) or a full refund, including any shipping costs you paid.
  • Do not attempt to repair or modify a defective product, as this voids eligibility.

Quality issues reported more than 30 days after delivery may be handled under the manufacturer's warranty. Many Intima products carry a 1–2 year manufacturer warranty; please refer to your product documentation for details.

Exchanges

We do not offer direct exchanges. If you wish to exchange an eligible unopened item for a different product, please return the original item following the process above and place a new order for the desired item. This ensures the fastest processing and avoids inventory conflicts.

Questions?

If you are unsure whether your item qualifies for return, or have any other questions, please contact us at support@intima-wellness.com. We are happy to help you determine the best course of action.

This return policy applies only to purchases made directly through intima-wellness.com. If you purchased through a third-party retailer, please refer to their return policy.
