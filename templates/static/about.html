Our Story

Intima was founded with a singular belief: intimate wellness deserves better.

We started Intima to redefine intimate wellness — an industry long dominated by clinical packaging, questionable materials, and an experience that felt more medical than personal. We envisioned a brand that treats intimate care products as objects of design and delight: body-safe by default, beautiful by choice, and delivered with the discretion you deserve.

Every product we create undergoes rigorous testing to meet medical-grade standards. Every package ships in a plain brown box with no external branding — because your privacy isn't an afterthought, it's our foundation. From material sourcing to customer support, we hold ourselves to one standard: would we trust it for our own bodies?

Safety First

Every product is tested to medical-grade standards. We use only body-safe materials like medical-grade silicone, tempered glass, and stainless steel — never anything we wouldn't use ourselves.

Design Driven

Beautiful objects that happen to be functional. We believe intimate products should look as good as they feel — sculptural forms, considered details, colors you'll love.

Privacy Obsessed

From packaging to payment, discretion is the default. Plain brown boxes, anonymous billing descriptors, and a privacy-first data policy mean your business stays yours.

Our Team

We're a small, passionate team of designers, engineers, and wellness advocates working remotely across the US and Europe. United by a shared belief that intimate wellness products deserve the same care and craft as any other premium category.
